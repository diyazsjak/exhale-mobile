Exhale · Feature proposal · Your own triggers

Name what sets it off,
in your own words.

Today a cigarette can be tagged with one of eight fixed triggers. People's real triggers are more specific: phone calls with mum, the bus stop, after a shift. This page proposes where a person creates their own trigger, where they manage the list, and how the rest of the app picks it up. Every frame is drawn with the app's own tokens and components.

A · In the moment

Add it where you already tag

Why here. The trigger is clearest in the seconds after logging. Sending someone to Settings to create it first means it never gets created.

Cost to logging. None. Logging stays one hold; tagging stays optional. The add chip is a quiet outlined pill at the end of the row, after the triggers people use.

One tap commits. Add creates the trigger and tags this cigarette with it. The Logged card's 8 s auto-dismiss pauses while the field is open.

B · Settings

One place to shape the list

Order is muscle memory. Chips stay in the order the person sets, never re-sorted by use, so “Coffee” is always where the thumb expects it.

Hide, don't delete. A trigger that tagged anything is hidden, not erased: its history stays in Rhythm. Built-ins can be hidden too (“I don't drive”).

Follows Settings' saving. Like “A pack” and “Saving for”: no Save button, changes save on the way back with a “Saved” toast.

C · Rhythm

Notice when “Other” means something

When. 5 or more logs tagged Other in the period shown, and only until the person names it or dismisses it. One card, no badge, no notification.

Retag carefully. “Other” can be several things, so the sheet lists those logs by time and the person picks which ones were this. None are picked by default.

Edit link. The Triggers card gets an “Edit ›” ExLink to the Triggers screen, so the list can be tidied where the chart is.

Rules

What a trigger can be

RuleProposal
Name1–20 characters after trimming, any script. Typed field is capped at 20 like goal names are at 40.
DuplicatesNot allowed against any trigger, shown or hidden, comparing case- and space-insensitively. A hidden match offers “Show Driving again” instead.
How many showUp to 12 shown at once (three rows of chips on a small phone). Adding a 13th asks to hide one first. Hidden ones are unlimited.
OrderSet by the person; new ones go to the end. Never re-sorted by frequency.
Built-insCan be hidden and reordered, not renamed (their if-lines are hand-written). “Other” always stays, last.
Hide vs removeAnything ever used can only be hidden; its tags stay and still count in Rhythm. Remove exists only for a trigger with no tags.
RenameCustom ones only. Past tags follow the new name (they point to the trigger, not the text).
ColourAll triggers use flare, as now. No per-trigger colour or emoji: colour means smoke vs recovery here, not categories.
Copy“Your own”, “Add”, “Hide from the list”, “Added ‘X’ to your triggers”. No exclamation marks, nothing about blame.

Considered

Ideas left for later, and why

Later

Onboarding step

“What usually sets it off?” as step 5. Adds a question before the person has logged anything, and onboarding is kept to four. Better: after 7 days, Rhythm suggests reviewing the list.

No

Sort chips by use

Puts the likely tag first, but chips would move between logs and break the fast tap. A fixed order the person sets wins.

No

Free text after every log

A text box instead of chips. Slow, uncountable, and it turns a two-second log into a journal.

Later

Places and times as triggers

“At work”, “8–9 am”. Time is already on the Smoke Clock; place needs location, which goes against “local, private, quiet”. Plain names cover it.

Later

Long-press a chip to edit

A shortcut from the Logged card into the edit sheet. Hidden gesture, easy to trigger while tagging. Fine as an extra once B exists.

Open questions

For you to decide

  1. Scope for v1. A + B together (recommended), or A only with hiding left for later?
  2. Built-ins. Can people rename built-ins too? It would drop their hand-written if-lines for the generic one.
  3. Cap. 12 shown chips, or fewer to keep the Logged card short on small phones?
  4. The “Other” nudge (C). Ship with v1 or after people have custom triggers for a while?
